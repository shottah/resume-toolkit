# Senior Analytics Engineer — Fernhill Health Partners

**Location:** Toronto, ON (hybrid, two days a week in the office)

Fernhill Health Partners runs community clinics across Ontario. Our data team
helps clinicians and operations leaders plan staff, beds and supplies. We are
hiring a Senior Analytics Engineer to own the models behind those decisions.

## What you'll do

- Build and maintain dbt models on Snowflake for patient flow, staffing and supply data
- Orchestrate pipelines with Airflow and keep them reliable and well tested
- Partner with clinical and operations leads to turn questions into dashboards in Looker
- Set standards for data quality testing and code review across the team
- Mentor analysts as they grow into analytics engineering

## What you bring

- 5+ years in analytics engineering or data analysis
- Strong SQL and working Python
- Hands-on experience with dbt, Airflow and Snowflake
- Experience modelling healthcare or other operational data
- Clear communication with non-technical partners

## Nice to have

- Experience with privacy rules for patient data
- Forecasting experience
