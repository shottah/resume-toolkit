COVER LETTER:
  - Active voice. No stock openers such as "I am writing to express my
    interest" or "I am excited to apply"; open with why this role and the
    strongest match.
  - Tie every claim to a number, a named system or a concrete outcome from
    source/.
  - If a sentence could appear in a letter to any company, rewrite it so it
    is about this job, or cut it.
  - Read notes.md in the same folder as the file you are writing. When its fit_gaps list is not empty,
    either name real experience from source/ that bridges a gap, or leave
    the gap unmentioned. Never apologise for a gap or promise to learn
    something.
  - Close with a short, specific next step.
